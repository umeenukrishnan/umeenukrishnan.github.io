Hello!😊

I’m Meenu, a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at Johns Hopkins University working at the intersection of computational mechanics and scientific machine learning — building solvers, surrogates, and optimization tools that help us understand how structures and materials really behave.

Physics, computation, and design — in one workflow.

From phase-field fracture and large-scale topology optimization during my Ph.D. at IIT Roorkee, to evolutionary deep neural networks and neural-operator benchmarks at Johns Hopkins, my work is about making hard mechanics problems solvable at scale.
